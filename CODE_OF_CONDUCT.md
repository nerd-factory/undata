# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Nerd Lab is for curious people, including — especially — the ones who do not yet know
the vocabulary. Anything that makes a beginner quieter is the thing we are here to
prevent.

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in this
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse,
inclusive, and healthy community.

## What good looks like

- Answering a basic question as if it were a good question, because it is one.
- Being specific about what is wrong, and never about who is wrong.
- Saying "I do not know" out loud, in public, where beginners can see someone senior do it.
- Accepting that someone else's approach can be different without being worse.
- Assuming inexperience before assuming bad faith.

## What is not acceptable

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing other people's private information — physical address, email address, or
  anything else — without their explicit permission.
- Condescension dressed as helpfulness. "Just read the docs", "this is trivial",
  "everyone knows that". If you would not say it to a colleague on their first day,
  do not type it here.
- Any other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## Scope

This applies in all Nerd Lab spaces — repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions —
and whenever someone is representing the community in public.

## Enforcement

Report anything, including things you are not sure about, to **mafi@mafizul.me**.

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The reporter's privacy
and security will be respected. You do not have to have the vocabulary for what happened
in order to report it, and you will not be asked to justify feeling uncomfortable.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may face
temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by other maintainers. Being useful to
the project is not a defence.

### Consequences

1. **Correction** — a private, written warning explaining what was wrong and why.
2. **Warning** — a warning with consequences for continued behaviour, including a period
   of no interaction with the people involved.
3. **Temporary ban** — a period of no public or private interaction with the community.
4. **Permanent ban** — for a pattern of violation, sustained harassment, or aggression
   toward individuals or groups.
